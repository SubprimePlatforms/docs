> Prepaid tokens, no long-term contracts, no volume minimums. You're only billed when a check actually completes.

deepidv runs on a **pay-per-use model** — buy tokens in USD, and each completed check deducts the corresponding amount from your balance. Tokens never expire and there are no volume minimums. On the Pay-Per-Use plan a \$19/month account maintenance fee applies from your second month.

### How it works

1. **Top up your balance** — purchase tokens through the [Admin Console](https://app.deepidv.com) under **Settings → Billing**
2. **Build a workflow** — combine the services you need in the [Workflows](/workflows/workflows) section
3. **Pay per check** — the total cost of a session is the sum of each enabled service

## Managing Your Balance

- Check your current token balance in the [Admin Console](https://app.deepidv.com)
- Top up anytime under **Settings → Billing**
- If your balance hits zero, session creation requests return a `402 Payment Required` error

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do tokens expire?">
    No. Once you purchase tokens, they stay in your account indefinitely. Use them whenever you need — there's no time pressure or monthly reset.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When am I charged?">
    You're billed when a session is created and the enabled services are triggered. Each service in the workflow is charged at its listed rate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there contracts or minimum spend requirements?">
    No annual contracts, no volume minimums, and no lock-ins — cancel anytime. The only recurring charge on Pay-Per-Use is the \$19/month account maintenance fee, which starts in your second month; your first month is free. Agent Suite tiers are billed monthly at their listed rate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does workflow pricing work?">
    Each workflow is made up of individual services. When a session runs, you're charged the sum of each enabled service that completes. Only toggle what you need — you're never paying for services you didn't use.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get volume discounts?">
    Yes. For high-volume use cases or enterprise needs, reach out to [sales@deepidv.com](mailto:sales@deepidv.com) to discuss custom pricing and volume rates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there setup fees or hidden costs?">
    No. There are no setup fees, no integration charges, and no hidden line items. The prices listed above are exactly what you pay per completed check.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Prices listed above are subject to change. For custom pricing, volume discounts, or enterprise plans, contact [sales@deepidv.com](mailto:sales@deepidv.com).
</Warning>
